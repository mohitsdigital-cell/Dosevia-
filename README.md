# Dosevia

Your Health. Your Family. One Connected Care.

Personal & Family Health Management Platform — medicine reminders, family care coordination, medical records, vitals tracking and wellness in one connected app.

## Status

Project scaffold — MVP development in progress.

## Tech Stack

- Mobile: Flutter (Dart), Riverpod, GoRouter, Drift/SQLite, flutter_local_notifications
- Initial Market: India (Android-first, English + Hindi)
